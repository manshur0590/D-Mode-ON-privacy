Privacy Policy

For D-Mode ON · Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how D-Mode ON (“the app”) handles information when you use the D-Mode ON focus timer. The app is published by Manshur Ali (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

Summary

D-Mode ON stores your sessions and preferences on your device. It has no account, developer-operated server, advertising, or analytics service. On Android, if you choose to enable the Accessibility service, the app checks locally whether a selected app is in the foreground so it can show your reminder. It does not read screen text or send this information to us.

Information handled by the app

  • Session information: the intention or session name, selected duration, start and end times, completion time, and the display names and package identifiers of apps you selected.
  • Preference: your light or dark appearance setting.
  • Android app selection and reminders: when you view the app picker, Android supplies launchable app names, package identifiers, and icons so you can choose apps. If you enable the Accessibility service, Android reports window-change events to the service. The service uses the foreground app's package identifier on-device to compare it with your selected apps and, when there is a match during an active session, show a dedication reminder. The service is configured not to retrieve screen content.

How information is stored and used

Session information and preferences are stored locally on your device using app-private storage. Android's reminder service also keeps the active session end time and selected package identifiers in app-private storage so it can provide reminders. We use this information only to run the timer, show reminders, and display your session history and insights.

We do not operate a service that receives this information. The app does not send your session details, selected apps, or foreground app identifiers to us. We do not use them for advertising, profiling, or analytics.

Sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share information handled by the app with third parties. The app does not integrate third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. Depending on your device and backup settings, the operating system or a backup provider may back up app-private data. Your device operating system and the app store may also process information under their own privacy policies and settings; those practices are controlled by their respective providers, not by D-Mode ON.

Retention and deletion

Session history and preferences are stored in the app's private device storage. The app does not currently provide an in-app control to erase all stored history. You can clear the app's storage or uninstall it using your device settings. If your operating system or backup provider has backed up app data, manage those backup copies through that provider's settings. On Android, you can also turn off the Accessibility service at any time in Android Settings. Ending a session clears the app's active Android reminder state; completed session history remains until deleted with app storage or the app is uninstalled.

Children's privacy

D-Mode ON is a general-purpose focus timer and is not designed to collect personal information from children. The app has no account or developer-operated data collection service.

Security

Session data is kept in app-private storage on your device. No method of local storage or electronic transmission is guaranteed to be completely secure. Protect access to your device using the security controls provided by its operating system.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features or data practices change. The current version will be published at the privacy policy address linked from the app's store listing. The date at the top of this page indicates when it was last updated.
